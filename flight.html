The Mission

The Mission

After months of design, construction, and testing, everything comes down to flight day. Our payload will ascend to approximately 100,000 feet aboard a high-altitude weather balloon, collect atmospheric data through the troposphere and into the stratosphere, then descend via parachute for recovery and data extraction.
