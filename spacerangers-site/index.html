Space Rangers is a student engineering team at Glendale Community College building a scientific payload for high-altitude balloon flight.

Our Mission

Real Science. Real Engineering.

Through NASA's ASCEND program, the Space Rangers are tackling the full engineering lifecycle — from initial research and payload design to construction, testing, and a real high-altitude flight. Every decision is documented, every result analyzed. This is what student science looks like when the stakes are real.

Engineering Process

From Concept to Stratosphere

01Complete

Research

Define the scientific objective, review prior work, and establish mission requirements.

02Complete

Design

Develop payload architecture, select components, and produce engineering drawings.

03In Progress

Construction

Fabricate and assemble the payload enclosure, sensors, and data systems.

04Upcoming

Testing

Validate performance under simulated conditions and iterate on design.

05Upcoming

Flight

Launch the payload aboard a high-altitude weather balloon and collect live data.

06Upcoming

Results

Analyze flight data, draw scientific conclusions, and present findings.
